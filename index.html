The place that stays with you

Welcome to the rhythm of the wetlands.

Mabamba Bay Wetland sits on the northern shore of Lake Victoria and is internationally known for its rich birdlife, including the sought-after shoebill. Quiet channels and papyrus stands make the journey part of the experience.
